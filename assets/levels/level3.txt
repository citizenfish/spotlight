; assets/levels/level3.txt -- Level 3, Rescue: the playtest building.
;
; THIS FILE IS THE SOURCE and spikes/scene.py is a view of it (issue #107).
; Until the light round (#121) its two rooms were the same bytes every run and
; every number in the vault was measured in them; the vault's "The Playtest
; Building" is the record. The light round made each a shell with a rolled
; interior; the plateau round (#137) authored the walls and **gave the building
; a third room**.
;
; **The third room is the round's fix for the plateau.** Levels 2 to 6 measured
; 84, 86, 91 and 83 per cent of the people rescued -- four level-ups a player
; would have felt as the same level renamed. The same seven clocks and six
; flies spread over three rooms instead of two takes a dark listener from 94
; per cent to 63, at no cost to the 48K frame at all: `worst_case()` sizes the
; swarm crowded into one room, so it scales with flies and people and not with
; rooms. The clock had been tightening for five levels against a walk too
; short for it to bite.
;
; Format: `level:`, `name:` and `building:` once, with the budget -- `blood:`,
; `spray:`, `lives:`, `magnet:` seconds, `wake: on|off`, `fade:` 1 or 2 --
; before the first `shape:` or `room:`; then `shape:` blocks, then `room:`
; blocks. `floor:` is yellow or cyan (adjacent rooms never share). A room
; carries `map:` (22 rows of 32) with `worker: x y blood` and `cleg: cx cy`;
; or `roll:` with `segments:`, `length:`, `pieces:`, `band:`, `away:`,
; `clegs:`, one `worker: blood` a person and one `shell:` a shell it may be
; built from. Every room has `searchlight: radius repeat|vary` (with `pace:`
; beside it), `start: x y` in pixels and `at: col row` on the plan;
; `light: left top width height` and `door: side rows-rows room` as authored.
; Blank lines and `;` comments go anywhere. (`spotlight:` and `torch:` went
; with the torch, #119; `mount:` went in #137 -- the housing's corner rotates
; round the building from the run's seed.)
