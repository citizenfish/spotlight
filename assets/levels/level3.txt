; THIS FILE IS THE SOURCE and spikes/scene.py is a view of it (issue #107).
; Until the light round (issue #121) the two rooms were the same bytes every
; run -- A's inner box with its one-cell door, the wall turned on its side,
; B's four desks -- and every number in the vault was measured in them; the
; vault's "The Playtest Building" is the record. Now each is a shell: A
; keeps its clocks, its three flies, its exit and its repeating beam; B its
; four people, its three flies, the light on the doorway home and a beam
; that varies. The walls, the furniture, the people's cells and the flies'
; starts roll from the run's seed (issue #120).
;
; Format: `level:` and `name:` once, with the budget -- `blood:`, `spray:`,
; `lives:`, `magnet:` seconds, `wake: on|off` -- before the first room;
; then `room:` blocks. `floor:` is yellow or cyan (adjacent rooms never
; share). A room carries `map:` (22 rows of 32: `#` wall, `.` floor, `D` the
; exit, `d` a doorway, the seven furniture characters `= | x [ ] c %`) with
; `worker: x y blood` and `cleg: cx cy`; or `roll:` with `segments:`,
; `length:`, `pieces:`, `band:` (min max), `away:`, `clegs:` and one
; `worker: blood` a person. Every room has `searchlight: radius repeat|vary`
; (with `pace:` and `mount:` beside it) and `start: x y` in pixels;
; `light: left top width height` and `door: side rows-rows room` as
; authored. The first room has the exit. Blank lines and `;` comments go
; anywhere. (`spotlight:` and `torch:` went with the torch, issue #119.)
