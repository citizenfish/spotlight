; assets/levels/level3.txt -- Level 3, Rescue: the playtest building.
;
; THIS FILE IS THE SOURCE and spikes/scene.py is a view of it (issue #107,
; The rooms AK). Two rooms, one doorway, no key: the building every phase-2
; baseline and every look round was measured in, byte for byte. The reasons
; the rooms are shaped as they are -- the inner box and its one-cell door, the
; wall turned on its side at column 24, the clock ladder, the doorway in the
; beam's path -- are in the vault's "The Playtest Building" and are not
; repeated here.
;
; Format: `level:` and `name:` once; then `room:` blocks. `floor:` is yellow
; or cyan (adjacent rooms never share). `map:` is 22 rows of 32: `#` wall,
; `.` floor, `D` the exit, `d` a doorway, and the seven furniture characters
; `= | x [ ] c %` on cells that would otherwise be wall (`%` a grating on
; floor). `worker: x y blood` in pixels and blood points; `cleg: cx cy`;
; `spotlight: cx cy power`; `light: left top width height`; `searchlight:
; radius repeat|vary`; `start: x y` in pixels; `door: side rows-rows room`.
; The level's budget -- `blood:`, `spray:`, `torch:`, `lives:` -- goes before
; the first room and defaults to the constants. The building starts in its
; first room. Blank lines and `;` comments go anywhere.
