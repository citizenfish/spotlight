; assets/levels/level2.txt -- Level 2, Infested: the corner.
;
; The second level (issues #111, #121, #137, and re-authored as **the corner**
; in #146). Three rooms: east, then south.
;
; **The first horizontal doorway in the game**, and the first room whose two
; doorways are at right angles rather than opposite. That is the whole lesson:
; until now "the way out" and "the way back" have been the two ends of the same
; wall, and a player has never had to tell them apart. Three rooms, so it costs
; nothing on any budget, and it is a teaching level where the mistake is cheap.
;
; The walls are authored and the contents roll (#137, rulings 2 to 5). A shell
; may reach the border, which is what makes a corridor or a chamber possible at
; all -- and it is gated on its bare walls by `swarming.strands_a_shell`, which
; is seed-independent, and on its doorways' landings by
; `roller.blocks_a_landing` (#144).
;
; **A shell belongs to an axis.** A wall across an east-west walk is furniture
; in a north-south one, and a wall standing in a doorway's own lane is refused.
; So `the wide dark`, whose only doorway is in the north wall, is built from the
; horizontal shells and not the vertical ones.
;
; The floor hue is `(col + row) & 1` of `at:` and is not authored (#145).
