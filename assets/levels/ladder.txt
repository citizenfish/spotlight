; assets/levels/ladder.txt -- the building Levels 4 and up are cut from.
;
; Issue #146. **Not a level**: `levels.levels()` globs `level*.txt` and this is
; not one, so nothing loads it by number. `levels._beyond` parses it, keeps the
; first N rooms for the level it is building, drops the doorways that led to the
; rest, and turns the dials.
;
; Nine rooms, two loops and three dead ends, lettered by their place on the plan:
;
;      A ----- B ----- E          loops:  A-B-D-C   and   B-E-F-D
;      |       |       |          spurs:  C-H,  D-G,  F-I
;      C ----- D ----- F
;      |       |       |
;      H       G       I
;
;      level  rooms  shape
;      4, 5   4      a ring                        A B C D
;      6, 7   5      the ring and one dead end     + E off B
;      8      7      two loops and a spur          + F, G
;      9+     9      two loops and three spurs     + H, I
;
; **Eight rooms is skipped**: seven is the last size at which every room holds
; somebody, so eight buys one empty leaf for nothing.
;
; **A ring of seven is not available and it is worth knowing why.** A grid graph
; is bipartite, so every cycle on the plan has an *even* length -- six or eight,
; never seven. Nine rooms is two four-cycles sharing an edge with three spurs
; hung below, which is what "a ring of seven with two spurs" becomes once that is
; respected.
;
; **Levels 1 to 3 are not on this ladder and are not supersets of one another.**
; They are three teaching shapes -- a row, a corner and a column -- each meeting
; one axis with the rest of the game held still. The superset chain starts here,
; at the ring.
;
; ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
; **Why a junction may only use four of the twelve shells**, which is the one
; thing about this building that was not foreseen:
;
; A doorway's *lane* -- its own span, five cells in from the wall it is cut
; through -- must hold no wall of the shell (#144), and from Level 5 a doorway
; may sit at any of six bands (#135). Take the union over four sides and six
; bands and the cells a wall may stand in are **columns 6 to 24 by rows 6 to
; 14** and nothing else. So **a wall in a room with doorways on both axes cannot
; reach a border**, which is the one thing the authored shells of #132 were for.
;
; `boxroom`, `vault`, `benches` and `pillars` live inside that rectangle. The
; other eight belong to an axis: `spine`, `spine-low`, `stubs` and `dogleg` to
; east-west, `rung`, `rung-low`, `ledges` and `piers` to north-south. The gate
; in `tests/test_spike_shells.py` refuses the rest, per room and per band.
; ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
;
; The dials are `levels._beyond`'s and are not authored here: the fly count is a
; **building total** dealt across the live rooms, so every `clegs:` below is nought
; and the number is the level's. The roster is the seven clocks below and never
; rises with the room count.

; **The roster's low end is 40 and not 30, and that is measured.** At four and
; five rooms the clock floor is 29 and 36, so these are the only buildings on the
; ladder where the shortest clocks stand as authored -- from seven rooms up the
; floor lifts everything below 50 anyway. A person who starts on 30 has sixty
; seconds, and **three bites cost about as much as sixteen seconds of bleeding**:
; the swarm bites whoever is following you, not only the player. The oracle
; cleared Level 5 on fourteen seeds of sixteen with 30 at the bottom and on all
; sixteen with 40, so a level perfect play could not finish became one it can.
; Seven people, and the roster never rises with the room count.
