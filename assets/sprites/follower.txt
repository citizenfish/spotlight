; follower.txt
;
; A worker you have freed, walking behind you, seen from the front.
; Three frames: a neutral stance and two strides.
;
; Why a third people sprite exists at all: raised arms MEAN "I still
; need reaching", and leaving them up on somebody already walking
; behind you is a lie the player would act on. A worker is drawn from
; their state, so a follower dropped back to waiting goes back to
; arms up on the frame they are dropped -- and keeps their stride,
; because the stride says which foot is forward and not what they are.
;
; REDRAWN 2026-09-12 (issue #72), IN ELEVATION, with the player and
; for the same reason -- see player.txt for the ruling. The same
; person as the waiting worker: the same head, the same neck and
; shoulders, the same legs and boots. Arms down the sides, hands at
; the hips, where a waiting worker's are up above the head.
;
; What tells it from the player, in the middle columns where the eye
; is: no lamp -- the top two rows are clear -- a round head on a neck
; instead of a helmet on a chin, and shoulders six wide where his are
; eight. The arms down its sides reach columns 0 and 7 in every frame,
; as the player's do; from 2026-09-11 to 2026-09-12 the plan-view
; follower kept clear of the edge columns and that was its tell, and
; in elevation it is not and is not asked to be. The contract compares
; every frame of it against every frame of the player and the worker,
; and a difference confined to the edge columns would fail it anyway.
;
; The walk is the player's: N A N B, a step every four pixels the
; trail carries it, `walk.Stride`, stepped in `Rescue.follow` where
; the trail places the figure. B is A in the mirror.
;
; 50 pixels in N, 47 in each stride.
;
; Authored here and generated into prototype/spikes/bitmaps_gen.py
; and spectrum/src/bitmaps.asm by tools/bitmaps.py, which also
; derives the one-pixel halo mask (issue #70). Comments start with
; ';' rather than '#', which is what a row of eight set pixels
; starts with -- the first version of this format used '#' and
; silently ate the player's shoulders.
