; spray.txt
;
; Flyspray on the ground: the droplets that mark a patch of poisoned floor.
;
; **It has to read differently from lit floor, and it has only pixels to do it
; with.** Sprayed ground is drawn in cyan while lit floor is white, but hue is
; the one thing an 8x8 cell cannot be relied on for -- a patch laid at the edge
; of the torch sits next to cells of every brightness, and colour blindness and
; a black-and-white television both exist. So the droplets are twice as dense
; as the lit stipple and, more to the point, **staggered**: two dots on every
; other row, the pairs shifted two pixels between rows, where the floor's dots
; line up in columns. A column of dots and a scatter of them are still different
; when both are the same colour.
;
; Drawn over the floor rather than instead of it -- the play area composites,
; nothing clears -- so a lit sprayed cell carries both patterns, which is why
; the offsets matter more than the count.
;
; **Not to be redrawn without a design decision.** The patch's shape is settled
; in the vault (issue #42 and issue #44 for the rebound), and its density is
; what tells a player at a glance how much of a doorway one charge plugged.
;
; It lived as hex in `spikes/spray.py` until issue #51 moved it here, so that
; the port gets these bytes as DEFB like every other bitmap and the drift test
; guards them.
