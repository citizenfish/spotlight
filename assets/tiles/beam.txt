; beam.txt
;
; The searchlight's pool, authored once as a picture (issue #128, from the
; user: *"it needs to look more circular and more like a searchlight"*).
;
; **The shape of the beam is a rule, not a decoration.** The ten-second
; magnet fires on exactly the cells the beam lights, so the drawn edge has
; to be the rule's edge. Both come from this file: the 49 blocks below are
; the 7x7 cells of the pool at radius 3, in reading order, cell 00 top-left
; and cell 48 bottom-right, and `tools/bitmaps.py` generates from them both
; the ring tiles the floor pass ORs in and the seven half-widths
; `sources.disc_widths` holds. A cell whose block is empty is outside the
; disc; a cell with pixels is inside it, whether or not the ring passes
; through that cell.
;
; **The circle is a midpoint circle of radius 22 pixels**, one pixel wide,
; centred in the 56x56 pool. Twenty-two and no other radius: the cells it
; touches are exactly the 37 of `3 5 7 7 7 5 3` and none outside them, so
; the picture and the rule agree cell for cell. 124 pixels of boundary
; against a pool of 116 dots -- the edge the player could not see before.
;
; **The interior is empty here.** What fills the pool is the floor's own
; stipple (`floor.txt`, four dots a cell lit and one dim), which is
; unchanged: the ring is drawn over it and under every sprite, wearing the
; cell's own ink and writing no attribute of its own. A denser fill was
; drawn and refused -- it reads blocky at 1:1 and drowns the people
; standing in the pool (*The searchlight's look* ruling 3).
;
; No dim variant: a remembered cell is remembered floor, and the ring is
; the beam being on a cell now.
